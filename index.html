Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where do you work?

We advise clients in the UK and Pakistan. Most consultations happen by phone, video call or email, so you can work with us from anywhere.

How much do your services cost?

It depends on the service and how much work is involved. After a first conversation we send a written quote, and nothing starts until you agree to it.

Are you a law firm?

No. We provide legal information and documentation support. If your matter needs a solicitor, barrister or advocate, we will tell you and can help you prepare for that meeting.

Can you help with more than one service?

Yes. Many clients need a combination, for example a business set-up with tax registration, or a university application with visa documents.
