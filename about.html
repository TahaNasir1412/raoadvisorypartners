About Rao Advisory Partners

An advisory practice built on clear explanations, careful paperwork and honest advice.

Who we are

Practical guidance for people with a lot at stake

Big decisions rarely come with clear instructions. A student weighing up universities, a founder registering a company, a family dealing with legal paperwork, or a candidate preparing for an election all face processes that are complex and unforgiving of mistakes.

Rao Advisory Partners brings these areas together under one roof. We explain your options, prepare what needs preparing, and stay with you until the job is done.

Our values

Clarity

We explain things in plain language, so you understand every decision before you make it.

Integrity

Our mission

How we work

Our process

  1. Initial conversation

    Tell us about your situation by phone or email. We listen, ask questions and confirm whether we can help.

  2. Clear proposal

    You receive a written outline of the work, the documents needed, timescales and fees before anything starts.

  3. Work in progress

    We prepare, review and submit what's needed, and keep you updated at each stage.

  4. Follow-through

    We stay on hand after delivery for questions, renewals and the next decision.
